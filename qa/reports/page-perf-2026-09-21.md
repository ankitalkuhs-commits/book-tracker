**ready** means the page's data has arrived: the API has been idle for 1 s after load. In this single-page app that is when the content actually appears.
**LCP**: good ≤ 2.5 s, ⚠️ ≤ 4 s, 🔴 > 4 s. **ready**: ⚠️ > 3 s, 🔴 > 6 s.

## Before / after Sprint 4D (deployed 2026-09-21, commit 7635aca)

Compared with `page-perf-2026-09-19` on the same pages, same profiles, same method.

| | desktop total (15 signed-in pages) | mobile total |
|---|---:|---:|
| before | 58.99 s | 91.56 s |
| after | **45.92 s** | **77.79 s** |
| change | **−13.07 s (−22%)**, mean 3.93 → 3.06 s | **−13.77 s (−15%)**, mean 6.10 → 5.19 s |

**Where it came from** — the pages that had serial stages:

| page | desktop | mobile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Friend's profile | 5.84 → 3.25 s | 7.79 → 5.17 s |
| Circle detail | 6.38 → 3.92 s | 8.89 → 5.71 s |
| Book detail | 4.71 → 2.33 s | 7.24 → 4.22 s |
| Notifications | 3.37 → 2.00 s | 4.70 → 3.77 s |
| Library | 3.11 → 2.08 s | 5.13 → 4.45 s |

**First paint improved further still**, because no page waits behind a full-screen "Loading…" any more: Library LCP 1.99 → 0.48 s, Notifications 2.42 → 0.46 s, Search 1.96 → 0.41 s, Circles 1.96 → 0.45 s (desktop).

**Two pages got slower, and it is a real effect rather than noise.** Home feed +0.59 s desktop / +1.26 s mobile, and Admin +0.49 s desktop. Both fire the heaviest calls on the site, and 4D starts all of them at once instead of in sequence. With `/books/recommendations` at 12 queries and `/notes/feed` at 8, and a pool of 5 connections in front of a database ~200 ms away (F-68), the requests now contend rather than queue. Parallelism did not create this cost; it exposed it.

**So the remaining work is the query count and the region**, not the front end:
- **Sprint 4E** cuts recommendations 12 → 4 and the feed 8 → 3, which is what makes the home feed benefit from 4D instead of suffering from it.
- **F-68** (API in Oregon, database in Singapore) remains the single largest cost on every page.
